# Security

Thanks for helping keep Kinetra and its users safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please don't report security problems in public issues, pull requests or on Discord.

Report them privately instead:

- through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (the "Report a vulnerability" button on this repository's Security tab), or
- by email to support@kinetra.live, with "Security" in the subject.

Please include:

- what the problem is and what an attacker could do with it;
- the steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept;
- the affected version and channel (stable or Canary), operating system, or service;
- how you'd like to be credited, if at all.

## What happens next

- We confirm that we've received your report within 3 business days.
- We investigate and keep you updated, usually within 10 business days.
- When a fix is ready, we release it and, with your permission, credit you in the release notes.

Please give us reasonable time to fix the problem before telling anyone else about it.

## Supported versions

Security fixes are made for the latest stable release and the current Canary build. Please update before reporting, if you can.

## Scope

In scope:

- the Kinetra desktop app (this repository);
- the kinetra-license crate;
- Kinetra's online services: kinetra.live, api.kinetra.live, account.kinetra.live and poll.kinetra.live.

Out of scope:

- denial-of-service attacks and load testing against our services;
- social engineering, phishing, or physical attacks;
- spam, or rate limits that work as intended;
- problems in third-party software or services we don't control (please report those to their owners);
- reports that only come from automated scanners, without a demonstrated impact.

## Rewards

We can't offer cash bounties, but we thank valid reports with Kinetra subscriptions, depending on severity:

- Low: credit in the release notes and on our security thanks page.
- Medium: 3 months of Kinetra Pro.
- High: 12 months of Kinetra Pro.
- Critical: 12 months of Kinetra Team.

Rewards are for the first valid, reproducible report of an in-scope issue that follows this policy. We decide the severity, and rewards are a thank-you, not a contract. Serious issues get a GitHub Security Advisory and a CVE ID, with credit to you if you'd like.

## Good-faith research

We won't take legal action against research done in good faith that follows this policy. That means:

- only test with your own accounts and data;
- never access, change or delete other people's data, and stop as soon as you reach any;
- don't disrupt our services or anyone's live production;
- report what you find to us privately and promptly.

Thank you for reporting responsibly.
